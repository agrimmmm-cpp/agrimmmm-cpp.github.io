01Flagship project · Oct 2026

ShadowAthlete

Personalized AI-powered sports analytics & training platform

Video processing
30s clip in ~10s
Pose landmarks
33 per frame
Repeat requests
~75% faster
Backend
15+ REST modules

Problem

Most athletes don't have a coach watching every rep. Phone footage is everywhere, but turning it into feedback that tracks improvement over time is hard.

What I built

A full-stack platform that analyses athlete video across 4 sports and generates a digital avatar that evolves as the athlete improves.

Technical highlights

  • Computer-vision pipeline built on YOLOv8, MMPose, OpenCV and PyTorch that extracts 33 body landmarks per frame and converts them into performance metrics.
  • FastAPI backend with 15+ REST modules on PostgreSQL, secured with JWT and validated end-to-end in Postman.
  • Redis caching cut response times on repeated requests by ~75%.
  • Backend services containerised with Docker, consumed by a React Native client; developed collaboratively on GitHub.

02Hackathon · Oct 2025

Re-Habit

AI motion-tracking web app for physiotherapy

Top 3 at StormHacks out of 250+ teams and 1,000+ students

Stroke-recovery exercises mostly happen at home, without a physiotherapist checking form. Re-Habit uses an ordinary camera to track a patient's movement in real time and guide them through each exercise.

  • Tracks joints with MediaPipe and OpenCV to count repetitions and detect incorrect movements, tuned to reduce false counts.
  • Dashboard measures movement symmetry, posture, and walking patterns and turns them into simple progress summaries.
  • Python
  • Flask
  • MediaPipe
  • OpenCV
  • Pose estimation
